# Logos

The wordmark is set in **Space Grotesk Medium**, frozen as outlines; the mark is an isometric cube: a regular hexagon outline plus three light inner edges meeting at the centre, so the volume reads at once instead of being implied. Never recolor outside teal-600 / cream-100. Clearspace: at least the height of the wordmark's x-height around the lockup. Don't crop, don't gradient, don't outline.

- `isometria-wordmark.svg` — lockup, wordmark + mark. Two inks: letters `#2B2D2B` (`cream-900`), mark `#2E6F6A` (`teal-600`). On cream surfaces.
- `isometria-wordmark-inverted.svg` — the same lockup in one ink, `#FEFDF9` (`cream-200`), for `teal-600` surfaces.
- `isometria-mark.svg` — mark only, cube 32×32 (outline 2, inner edges 0.85) — also the favicon. One ink, `#2E6F6A` (`teal-600`).
- `isometria-mark-inverted.svg` — mark only, one ink, `#FEFDF9` (`cream-200`), for `teal-600` surfaces.

## Cube edges

The three inner edges (centre → upper-left, upper-right and bottom vertices) are drawn in the **same ink** as the outline, never with opacity or a second colour. They stay light through stroke weight alone: about 0.43× the outline (0.85 on the 2-unit mark, 0.7 on the 1.5-unit lockup). Round caps; the edges sit under the outline.
